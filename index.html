01 / THE FIRST ASSIGNMENT

One hand.
Five tiny sensors.
Room to explore.

We imagine a musical instrument you play by moving your fingers.

Before we explore the sounds, we need to explore the hand. Create a wearable system that holds five motion sensors, one on each finger, allows natural finger movement, and provides reliable sensor data.

The physical design is entirely open. We’d love to see the ideas you develop to meet this challenge.

02 / THE STARTING POINT

Small electronics.
Big possibilities.

The first assignment is the wearable sensor system. It should hold five motion sensors, one on each finger, allow natural finger movement, and provide reliable sensor data. Musical synthesis software comes later; it isn’t part of this assignment.

What needs figuring out?

How can the system hold all five sensors securely while your fingers move naturally? How reliably can it capture data as you move your hand?

The physical design is entirely open — develop your own ideas. Show us what you try, how freely the hand moves, and how reliable the readings are. Tell us what you learn and what needs another attempt.

Proposed reference components

Sensors5 × LSM6DSV16X motion sensors, one per finger
ConnectivityQwiic / JST-SH connectivity between the electronics
ControllerESP32 with Qwiic connectivity to collect the sensor readings
Sensor interfacePCA9548A eight-channel I²C multiplexer to connect and read the five sensors
Wearable designAn entirely open physical design that holds one sensor on each finger, allows natural movement, and provides reliable sensor data

Our reference electronics. Your physical design.

Please plan to work with these reference electronics. If you have a compelling reason to use different components, we’re happy to hear it. Discuss any changes with us and get approval before purchasing. The physical design is entirely open.
